# Brasaland Digital Project Brief

## Company Identity

Brasaland is a grilled food restaurant chain founded in Medellin, Colombia in 2008. The company operates 14 company-owned locations across Colombia and the United States, with its Florida operations coordinated from Miami. Brasaland employs approximately 115 people and generates about $6M in annual revenue.

Mariana Restrepo, daughter of the founder, has served as CEO since 2019. She led the company into the US market and now sponsors Brasaland Digital: the internal team responsible for building modern systems without weakening the brand's consistency, warmth, or kitchen speed.

## Core Problem

Brasaland is running a two-country restaurant chain with tools suited to a single local restaurant. The company operates across Colombia and Florida, two currencies (COP and USD), and two languages (Spanish and English), but lacks real-time data, unified internal systems, integrated point-of-sale feeds, consolidated purchasing visibility, and shared operational dashboards.

The practical impact is visible across the business: ingredient orders happen through WhatsApp or phone calls, leadership waits for weekly PDFs, loyalty data is lost on physical stamp cards, HR workflows are manual, and recipe updates travel slowly across 14 locations.

## Strategic Objectives

- Restaurant Operations, led by Felipe Guerrero: real-time location sales dashboards, ingredient ordering based on historical demand and current stock, and alerts when locations show no sales during opening hours.
- Procurement and Suppliers, led by Lucia Fernandez: supplier management with price history, purchasing visibility across Colombia and Florida, and alerts for material price changes.
- Marketing and Digital Experience, led by Camila Ospina: digital ordering, Brasa Points loyalty, CRM records with order history and preferences, and personalized menu recommendations.
- People and Culture, led by Ashley Turner: HR portal for holiday requests, absence management, onboarding automation, and KPI dashboards segmented by country.
- Training and Quality Standards, led by Jake Morrison: searchable recipe catalog, structured staff onboarding paths, centralized recipe update publishing, and optional Spanish-English support from a single source of truth.
- Technology, led by Nicolas Park: central Brasaland API for locations, menus, sales, customers, and suppliers; real-time location telemetry; and data pipelines feeding operations, marketing, finance, and executive dashboards.
- Executive Direction, led by Mariana Restrepo: executive dashboard for total chain sales in USD and COP, natural-language AI assistant access, and automated Monday morning reporting.
